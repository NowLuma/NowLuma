# Console design contract

The visual hierarchy is Notion-first (`design (1).md`) with sparse stationary background dots/lines and genuine optical chrome (`design.md`). Quantized identity remains confined to its shared mark; the references never change business state, permissions or capability claims.

- **Session → connection → results:** overview leads with the selected QQ session and the existing `nextStep` action, followed by saved instruments and reading widgets. Locally recognized identity is not proof of login, and an available endpoint is not proof of a robot reply. Loading, errors, explicit PID selection and account isolation retain their existing owners. The overview calls the shared guarded load directly; it does not add a second kernel choice or confirmation. An optional `manualStopped` flag suppresses idle reattachment prompts, never errors or in-flight work. A stopped workflow clears its transient dialogs.
- **One primary navigation:** the persistent dock owns the six canonical routes. Its saved order/visibility and pinned routes remain intact. The masthead opens **工作区管理** directly for keyboard/drag reordering, visibility, navigation explanations, hidden-page access, card editing and updates; **更多操作** holds session actions. Management does not repeat the visible route list. Closing it restores focus to the management trigger; entering kiosk closes it without resetting the layout.
- **Stable content:** paper/ink tokens, quiet borders and stationary reading surfaces form the base. Business rows and document actions do not lift or scale. Widget IDs, coordinates, visibility and configuration are unchanged; the existing catalogue remains the sole default source. Account rows offer explicit account-aware connection shortcuts. Decorative layers never replace business content, error details or focus indicators.
- **Optical chrome:** `GlassMaterial` stays behind unfiltered labels and focus rings. `GlassSurface` is the shared bounded composition for action, navigation, search and menu surfaces. Glass refracts real page content, not an exposed artificial stripe scene. Geometry is bounded to 768 × 480 CSS pixels of area, with a 256-pixel maximum raster side. At most 16 geometry rasters are cached across reopened surfaces; resize/preferences coalesce into one update, never a continuous canvas/filter loop. Local press, selection and menu responses retain existing Motion/CSS transitions. Pointer glints retarget persistent springs for position and opacity, preserving motion through entry, exit and rapid reversal; unmount destroys the springs and subscriptions. Low mode keeps short responses and a polished frosted edge.
- **Renderer truth:** `refracted` means SVG displacement was installed for Chromium-family CSS backdrop URL support, not that a pixel test passed. Safari/Firefox, unsupported/oversized surfaces and raster failures use `frosted`; high contrast/reduced transparency use opaque `solid`. Host-managed appearance hides the material and retains its surface rules. Static refraction may remain under reduced motion: optical displacement is not an animation. No free-GPU-cost or measured-animation-quality claim follows from unit tests or a screenshot.
- **Identity:** the canonical SVG is sampled once onto a bounded 160 × 224 raster and a 40 × 56 brightness grid. Exactly four CSS-pixel stroke widths render as four grouped SVG paths; background strokes are subdued separately so they cannot obscure the source-derived mark. Login and first setup share the source, neutral paper and compact system-font copy; no decorative lens covers the mark, and no second brand master, per-frame canvas or independent login-effects state exists.
- **Keyboard editing:** focused desktop handles use Arrow movement, Shift+Arrow resizing and Delete hiding through the existing GridStack/change writer. Bounds/minimum dimensions and durable widget config are retained; hiding restores focus to a surviving handle or the gallery. Mobile edit handles use ArrowUp/Down through the original one-dimensional order writer, with unchanged IDs/visibility/shared config. Pointer behavior and the separate mobile layout remain intact.
- **Manual effects:** `disableMotion` is the persistent on/off owner. OOBE and Settings share explicit motion and full/frosted-glass choices; legacy `auto` quality normalizes to `high`, keeping unrelated settings. No frame sampler changes the UI automatically. OS/manual reduction, transparency, contrast and host rules retain precedence. OOBE previews are reversible and the real settings save must settle before advancing; developer replay writes nothing. Controls retain 44px targets, labels, keyboard equivalents and visible focus.
- **Feedback placement:** first setup owns a bounded in-flow feedback row, not an overlay on its confirmation/save buttons. The same viewport and stable portal container move back to the floating workspace placement afterward; result countdowns, dismissal, focus and in-flight operations remain with their original owners.

Validation is frontend typecheck/lint, effect-control and guarded-flow interactions, material cache/observer lifecycle, canonical line identity, navigation management and persistence regressions, the full frontend suite, production build and demo build. Real Chromium refraction, Safari/Firefox fallback, 375/768/1440 layouts, keyboard/screen-reader behavior and frame continuity require parent-owned browser acceptance. These checks do not prove QQ delivery, robot replies or account login.
